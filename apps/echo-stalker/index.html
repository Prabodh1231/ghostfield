Echo Stalker

You cannot see. Clap to send a sound wave. Walls glow where it hits. It hears you too.

  • W A S D or arrows: walk (your steps make soft sounds; hold Shift to sneak silently)
  • Space: loud clap. Shows the room, but the monster hears it
  • Click: throw a pebble. The sound lands where you click, to lure the monster away
  • Grey dotted zones soak up sound. Hide in them. Reach the faint pulsing exit.
  • Teal rings are your sounds. Red dashed rings are the monster's footsteps.

Needs a keyboard and a mouse. Turn your sound on.

Same wave engine as the Wi-Fi and bass apps. It is a flat (2D) model of sound, so it shows the idea, not exact acoustics.
